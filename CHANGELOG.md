# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Death points are now named after the biome you died in (e.g. `death-plains`, `death-deep-dark`) and never get renamed.
- `/crumb go death` always takes you to your most recent death.
- Death points saved in 1.0.0 keep their current `death-N` name.

## 1.0.0

- First release: `/crumb save`, `/crumb go`, `/crumb stop`, `/crumb delete`, and `/crumb` to list.
- On-screen arrow with distance and height difference.
- Auto-saves your last 5 death points.
